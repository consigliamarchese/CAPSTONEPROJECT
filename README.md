# Market Share Analysis Report

Final Capstone Project developed for the Master in Data Analytics.

## Project Overview

This project was developed using Power BI and focuses on the analysis of market performance, competitive positioning, and growth opportunities for the fictional manufacturing company VanArsdel Ltd.

The goal of the analysis is to identify trends in market share, evaluate business performance, and compare the company against its main competitors through interactive dashboards and KPI-driven insights.

The analysis specifically focuses on the year 2014, during which a significant decline in market share was identified in June.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze VanArsdel’s market performance
- Monitor sales and revenue trends
- Evaluate brand sentiment over time
- Identify competitive threats and growth opportunities
- Compare competitors by market share and total units sold
- Explore regional and segment-based performance

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling

---

## Dashboard Structure

### 1. Market Performance Overview

This page provides a general overview of VanArsdel’s performance through key KPIs and interactive visualizations.

#### Main KPIs
- Unit Market Share
- Total Revenue
- Sentiment Score

#### Main Visualizations
- Total Units by Month
- Sentiment by Month
- Unit Market Share Rolling 12 Months
- Interactive filters by Region and Sentiment

#### Key Insight
A noticeable decline in market share was identified during June 2014, highlighting a potential loss of competitiveness.

---

### 2. Competitors & Growth Opportunities

This section focuses on competitor analysis and market opportunities.

#### Main Visualizations
- Top 5 competitors by Total Units
- Average Score comparison
- Total Units by Segment
- Total Units by Rolling Period and Region

#### Key Insight
The analysis highlights differences in market positioning between competitors and identifies segments and regions with potential growth opportunities for VanArsdel.
